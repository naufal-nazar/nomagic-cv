# Adapting to situations

The six layers stay the same; what changes is the top third and how history is framed. "Practice" means common professional guidance without a dedicated study.

| Situation | Adjustment | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Early career | One page; education and projects near the top; numbers from coursework, internships or volunteering | ResumeGo (length); practice |
| Experienced (8+ years) | Two pages; last 10-15 years in detail, older roles one line each | ResumeGo; practice |
| Newcomer with foreign experience | Local experience visible and first by date; lift foreign results into summary and highlights; name globally known employers and standards | Oreopoulos |
| Current role junior to past roles | Target title in headline and a highlights block from stronger roles; keep chronological order | Ladders; practice |
| Career changer | Headline = target role; summary bridges transferable results; relevant projects before experience | Practice |
| Employment gap | Show months and years; short gaps need no note; long ones get a one-line neutral entry (study, caregiving, relocation) | Kroft et al.; Resume Genius figure pending re-check |
| Leaving a job or serving notice | Keep "Present" until the last day, then add the end month. Never mention notice or reasons | Practice; honesty guardrail |
| Regulated or technical fields | Credentials, licences, standards and named systems carry the search; list in full with acronyms | Jobscan |
| Several target roles | One master per role family, one tailored copy per posting; never one generic CV | ResumeGo (tailoring); Resume Now figure pending re-check |
